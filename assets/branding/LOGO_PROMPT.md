# MovieSeat logo

File: `assets/branding/movieseat-logo.png`

Created with the built-in image generation tool. Transparent PNG. Concept: a cinema armchair combined with a ticket and play symbol, alongside the MovieSeat wordmark in the app's indigo/violet palette.

## Final prompt

Use-case: logo-brand. Design one polished original logo for MovieSeat, a mobile cinema ticket and seat reservation app. A distinctive compact symbol combining a front-facing cinema armchair with the silhouette of a movie ticket; a small clean play-triangle cutout integrated into the chair back, with balanced simple bold geometric shapes. Palette: rich indigo #312E81 and vivid violet #8B5CF6 with a restrained violet gradient on the symbol. Beside the symbol, the exact wordmark MovieSeat in a refined bold modern rounded sans-serif, perfect spelling and capitalization, deep indigo lettering, precise clean kerning. One horizontal logo lockup centered with generous transparent padding, symbol on left, wordmark on right. Logo should be recognizable at small mobile UI sizes. Flat, crisp vector-like graphic delivered as a high-resolution PNG with genuinely transparent background. No mockup, no 3D, no scene, no paper, no watermark, no tagline, no extra text, no extra logo variations. Professional friendly cinema identity, elegant and uncluttered.
